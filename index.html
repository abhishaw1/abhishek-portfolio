01 / A little about me

Tech is my thing. Helping is my nature.

I’ve always enjoyed technology—not only using it, but poking around to understand how it works. I get a kick out of finding the cause of an issue, learning something new, and getting things back on track.

The best part of IT support is the human side: helping someone who’s stuck, being patient with questions, and explaining a fix in a way that makes sense. I believe people should feel comfortable asking for help, never embarrassed by it.

02 / My approach

Good support is human first.

The way I try to show up—whether it’s a tricky technical issue or a simple question.

01 — LISTEN

Understand before fixing

I listen, ask questions, and try to understand what the person is actually experiencing instead of jumping to assumptions.

02 — FIGURE IT OUT

Stay curious

I like tracing issues, testing possible causes, and learning what’s happening behind the scenes. If I don't know yet, I work to find out.

03 — SHARE

Make tech less intimidating

I explain without unnecessary jargon, document useful fixes, and share what I learn so the next time is easier.
